Castly

Mirror your phone to a TV with sound. No cables, no ads, no account.

Download phone app Download TV app

What it does

  • Show your whole screen, or just one app, on the TV
  • Play a video, song or photo on the TV without mirroring
  • Use your phone as a TV remote, with touchpad, scroll and keyboard
  • Send APKs and files to the TV
  • Watch your TV screen on your phone

Install

  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it. Allow Install unknown apps when asked.
  2. On the TV, open this website's home page. It shows a code and a QR code.
  3. In Castly choose Connect to TV, then Code, and type the code, or scan the QR.
  4. Tap the big button and choose what to show.

Your phone and TV must be on the same Wi‑Fi. Video and sound go straight from the phone to the TV and never pass through this website.
